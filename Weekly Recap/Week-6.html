Week 6 Matchup Recap & Roast Report

The Week 6 Shakeup: The final undefeated team in the RNFF has fallen. Bye weeks and sudden offensive dead zones shook the leaderboard to its core. Between heavy-volume running backs hitting defensive walls and managers watching waiver acquisitions immediately make or break matchups, Week 6 rewarded squads with real tactical depth and punished those auto-piloting on name value alone.
